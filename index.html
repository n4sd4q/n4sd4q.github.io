The packages

Each one is a paclet, installed with one line; its showcase goes through everything it does, and its manual describes every function.

FunctionalDiagrams

Feynman diagrams from functional derivatives

Perturbation theory and effective actions as one does them on paper: Luttinger–Ward, parquet, Hedin, Matsubara sums, dimensional regularization and one-loop QED, every diagram drawn.

  • version 2.6.0
  • 875 tests
  • GPL-3.0-or-later
WebsiteShowcaseManualGitHub

FermiWick

Fermion operators, as symbols and as matrices

Normal ordering with compact local operators, Wick's theorem in Gaussian states declared by their symmetries, mean field of any order, Gutzwiller projectors, and exact diagonalisation on symmetry sectors.

  • version 0.2.0
  • 85 tests
  • GPL-3.0-or-later
